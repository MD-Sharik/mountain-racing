What's in the game

Real physics driving

Two pedals, gas and brake. Balance your car over endless hills without landing on your head.

Six vehicles

From the Rookie Buggy to the Star Rover. Each one handles differently and has four upgrades.

Five worlds

Green Valley, Dusty Canyon, icy Frost Peak, low-gravity Luna Base and Magma Ridge.

Six drivers

Unlock drivers with perks such as extra coins, better fuel economy or one free head bump.

Stunts pay

Flips and long air time earn bonus coins. Fuel cans keep the run alive.

Always something to do

Missions, daily rewards and levels. Free to play, supported by ads.
