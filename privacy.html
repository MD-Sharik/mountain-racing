Privacy Policy

Effective date: 15 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Mountain Racing mobile game ("the game", "we") handles and why. The game is free to play and is supported by advertising.

1. Information we collect ourselves

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.

Your game progress (coins, gems, unlocked vehicles, drivers and stages, upgrades, best distances, missions, daily reward status, sound setting and simple play statistics used to pace the game) is stored only on your device. It is not sent to us. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes it.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads using Google AdMob, a service provided by Google. To deliver and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, Google's software inside the game may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID;
  • your IP address, which can indicate your approximate location;
  • device and app information such as device model, operating system version, language and app version;
  • information about the ads shown to you and how you interact with them;
  • diagnostic information such as crash and performance data related to ads.

Google may use this information to show you personalised ads, depending on your settings and the laws where you live. Google acts under its own privacy policy. You can read how Google uses information from apps that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

3. Your choices

  • Personalised ads: on Android, open Settings, then Google, then Ads, to reset or delete your advertising ID or to opt out of ad personalisation.
  • Rewarded ads are optional: you choose whether to watch an ad in exchange for an in-game reward.
  • Delete your game data: uninstall the game, or clear its storage in your device settings.

4. Children

The game is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us using the details below.

5. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Apart from the advertising processing by Google described above, we do not share information from the game with third parties.

6. Security and retention

Because your progress stays on your device, we do not hold a copy of it. Information processed by Google for advertising is kept and protected under Google's own policies.

7. Changes to this policy

If the game changes in a way that affects how information is handled, we will update this page and its effective date. Continuing to play after an update means you accept the updated policy.

8. Contact

Questions or requests about this policy can be sent by opening an issue at github.com/MD-Sharik/mountain-racing/issues.
